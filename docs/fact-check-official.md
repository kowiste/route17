# Fact-check of the official explanations (`questions/official-2026/`)

Agent I, 2026-11-20. All 300 entries of `questions/official-2026/questions.json` were read with their options, the manual's marked answer, our explanation and the difficulty. Only the `explanation` and `difficulty` fields were touched; every question, option, answer, type and ID is exactly as the manual has it (a script check compared them before and after). Constitutional articles were checked against the consolidated text on boe.es; other facts against official sites (INE, BOE, La Moncloa, ministries, IPC) and encyclopaedias. The log names questions by ID only.

## Summary

- 20 explanations changed, 2 difficulties changed (22 entries), out of 300.
- No explanation contradicts its marked answer any more. The one marked answer that the current data no longer supports (5046) keeps the manual's answer; its explanation now says what the manual expects and what the data says today.
- Constitutional reforms: the Constitución de 1978 has been reformed four times (1992 art. 13.2, 2011 art. 135, 2024 art. 49, 2026 art. 69.3, Formentera's own senator, BOE 20 May 2026). No official explanation counts or lists the reforms. 1016 (the Senate, art. 69) still reads correctly after the 2026 reform; 1056 (male preference in art. 57) is still the text in force.
- Difficulty: the 166 / 97 / 37 split was sensible; two entries moved in opposite directions (below), so the split stays 166 / 97 / 37.
