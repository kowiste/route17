- **I (official explanations)**: the 2026 manual may still say the Constitution has been reformed three times. It has been reformed four times since 20 May 2026 (art. 69.3, Formentera); an explanation that counts the reforms should say so.
- Nothing was left unverified that affects an answer. Customs with no single source (meal times, two kisses, the Feria de Abril "unas dos semanas después de la Semana Santa") are worded as "normalmente" / "suele", which is accurate.
